# Golden UI v3 Visual Director review

## Scope and limits

Reviewed complete native Preview captures and full reading canvases for the five fresh reports, plus the remapped follow-up and blank equivalent. Each is compared with same-genre CHG-181 R2 Preview captures under the same visual read; the fresh supply-chain holdout is compared directionally with the prior Order/Returns holdout because the data shapes differ. Original captures are untouched; review PDFs only place each capture at a fit-to-page scale for comparison. The R1/R2 source comparison is included to check whether earlier dead-band and stacking problems returned.

The review applies the v3 task-grounded Visual Director across character, typography, density, hierarchy, spacing, surfaces, borders/radii, color semantics, emphasis, data density, content tone and responsive composition. It separates visible pixels from effects of the supported authoring tasks. It does not claim controlled human preference, native-device accessibility, field performance, or company-managed production certification.

## Whole-report reading

- **Executive KPI / business review.** Attention first goes to the two-line renewal warning and large 14.1M / 92.9% metrics. The report says renewal quality improved but the 84.7% public-sector cohort threatens expansion. It supports gating that budget until named renewals sign. The aggregate 92.9% and larger metrics can compete with the more decision-relevant public-sector evidence, which is relegated to the table. The decision text is present and clear, but follows the familiar amber-card pattern.
- **Semiconductor RCA.** Attention first goes to the WQ-7314 edge-yield headline and 91.8% value. The wafer map, 98.0% paired reference, SPC/distribution evidence and process sequence support containment pending endpoint calibration and a matched run. The report explicitly preserves causal uncertainty. The metric/card group and generic plots share visual weight; the action is near the bottom.
- **Experiment decision.** Attention first goes to the 24.8% treatment value and diverging weekly trend. The content supports only a staged rollout with a +0.15-point support-contact stop and says persistence is unproven. The trend and box plot dominate the compact decision/evidence prose; novelty and sample caveats require reading the narrative.
- **Dense technical operating review.** Attention goes to 96.2% yield and the SPC chart. The report says 7.4 hours of downtime and control checks keep release conditional. Large blank areas around the analysis group and very small supporting chart annotations reduce simultaneous evidence scanning. The decision is clear but visually lower than metrics/chart.
- **Fresh supply-chain holdout.** Attention goes to the 2.7% headroom and 116-day actuator lead. The report supports reserving qualified stock while the dated allocation is unresolved. The long headline and next step wrap at 320 px; large comparison numbers split across lines.
- **Remapped service-incident follow-up.** Attention goes to the incident headline, 22-hour customer impact and 42→15 P1 trend. It supports keeping rollout gated until matched-shard confirmation. Source numbers and identity do not leak; the evidence and risk language remain consistent. It uses the same section/card visual grammar as the blank equivalent.

## Comparative visual judgement

The fresh reports remain populated and sectioned; the earlier CHG-181 R1 large spacing/stacking problems visible in its comparison are not the dominant pattern in fresh R2 captures. Against accepted CHG-181 R2, however, the fresh reports offer no obvious improvement in purposeful focal hierarchy, typographic character, group spacing, feature/support treatment, density control or genre-specific art direction. Repetition creates a reliable interface but makes complete reports read like a family of correctly arranged widgets. This fails the specific principal-designer-quality bar even though the web Preview is generally legible and the decision narrative is coherent. No numeric aesthetic score was assigned.

## Weak scenes and evidence limits

The technical review was read with long batch identifiers and six SPC samples at 320 px. The supply-chain holdout uses multi-digit values, five-column supplier evidence and a four-step flow at 320 px. The narrow captures show numeric wrapping and diagram compression. The table has horizontal overflow diagnostics and a scrollable region, but its partially visible columns need an obvious user-facing affordance. Ambiguous remap asks for explicit choice and enables Apply only afterward. The incompatible case stays blocked, but its diagnostic is not sufficiently specific.

No claim is made about native mobile hardware, screen-reader usability, user preference, task time in a field population or field performance. Recorded action totals describe this deterministic run only; they are not a universal time SLA.
