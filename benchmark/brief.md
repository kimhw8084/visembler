# CHG-173 R6 benchmark brief

Six complete reports were created or remixed through Report Hub, component library, Data First, Inspector, composition recipe, Preview and export flows. Data and outputs are run-owned. Fresh holdouts E and F use Bayshore membrane recovery and Harbor Reach ferry operations; neither copies R5 or CHG-285 fixtures.

| ID | Report | Audience and decision | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Lakeside renewal gate | Commercial/service leads decide whether to release two hires. | Renewal trend, metrics, regional table and conditional gate. |
| B | TSV copper-fill RCA | Yield engineer decides containment pending chamber/reference checks. | Wafer map, SPC, Box Plot, evidence table, finding, status and Process Flow. |
| C | Aster guided setup | Growth/customer-success decide staged rollout. | Twelve-week grouped trend, cohort evidence and guardrails. |
| D | Cell 3 furnace release | Operations/maintenance/Quality decide supervised release. | Twelve shifts, SPC, dense handoff table, owner and status. |
| E | Bayshore membrane recovery (fresh dense holdout) | Water-quality lead decides whether to continue one recovery cycle. | Eight stages, sample points, metrics, table, concise flow and stop criteria. |
| F | Harbor Reach ferry (fresh sparse holdout) | Service planner decides one earlier departure trial. | Four pier values, queue guardrail, rationale and reversible stop condition. |

All saved models were reloaded before in-app Preview. Editable PPTX and SVG files were generated for A–F. Further Product proof stopped after VIS-R6-001; PPTX XML inspection and A–F canonical re-import were not run.
