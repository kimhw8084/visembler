# Visual Director review — six complete reports

## Shared product identity

All six reports use Visembler’s shared typography, blue/neutral palette, section numbering, card surfaces, semantic component roles, and data-aware layouts. The same grammar supports distinct reading paths: executive decision, spatial RCA, controlled experiment, operations, dense field readiness, and sparse route activation. The notes below judge task completion and populated pixels, not personal taste.

## A — FY26 Northline Network Retention Decision

- **Person, context, task, decision:** A finance executive and market owner deciding whether to release the next FY26 acquisition-spend tranche at quarter close; hold it until Metro East renewals meet 93.0%.
- **Current truth:** Portfolio renewal is 91.4%; eight-quarter trend rises but remains below threshold. Central and Mountain are improving, Metro East is below median, and two large renewals can move the denominator.
- **Character / negative identity:** A budget-gating brief; it avoids becoming a celebratory acquisition dashboard or a customer-level churn ledger.
- **Density and hierarchy:** Moderate. The headline and renewal KPI lead; market contribution distribution, renewal trend, account-group evidence, then risk and next step support the decision.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** One large statement, compact context, standard metric/comparison numerals, smaller chart/table labels. Metrics are grouped; two analytical views sit beside the evidence table; semantic callout and next-step surfaces close the read.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** Renewal is the feature measure; ARR and comparison support it. Blue marks the primary trend; box-plot series colors separate market groups without changing meaning. The market table holds identifiers and gate states.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Conditional and measured. On mobile, the statement, evidence, decision, and next step become a single reading column; table columns remain reachable through keyboard/touch overflow.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** A reader could overstate portfolio certainty from two large renewals; the report names denominator sensitivity. The PPTX label-interval finding is separately recorded and materially affects this report’s exported trend labels.

## B — QF-8419 Microbump Yield RCA

- **Person, context, task, decision:** Device engineering and quality leaders deciding lot release after an RFX-12 excursion; contain QF-8419 until the paired QF-8421 split confirms recovery.
- **Current truth:** The die cluster is localized, the post-handoff resistance/SPC shift aligns, and carrier-clean age remains a competing explanation. The mechanism is probable, not proven.
- **Character / negative identity:** An investigation record with spatial evidence and an explicit hypothesis conflict; it avoids presenting a single-cause verdict as settled.
- **Density and hierarchy:** High. Scope and population lead into the process path, paired distribution, evidence table, competing-mechanism card, spatial map, and subgroup trace.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** Strong headline and compact lot/station context; evidence labels are smaller. Spatial and statistical evidence are separate groups, and the competing explanation has its own surface.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** Wafer location is primary; subgroup series and paired-lot distribution support it. The RFX-12/paired lot identities remain explicit in visible text and chart series; table values retain lot/station identifiers.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Cautious and release-oriented. The 320px flow stacks in order; wafer geometry stays within its disk, and the evidence table discloses its horizontal navigation.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** A reader might treat spatial correlation as causation; the competing CC-392 explanation and split-lot condition remain visible.

## C — North Basin Membrane Pilot Decision

- **Person, context, task, decision:** A treatment pilot lead deciding whether to expand staged flux; authorize one additional basin for six weeks, with a paired turbidity stop rule.
- **Current truth:** Ten paired blocks favor staged flux on residual turbidity and median retention; cold-water weeks widen both arms, and two samples were imputed. The pilot does not support a seasonal reliability claim.
- **Character / negative identity:** A controlled-experiment decision memo; it avoids a generic chart gallery and a network-wide deployment claim.
- **Density and hierarchy:** Medium-high. Headline, treatment outcome, grouped two-arm time series, flux distribution, operating table, rationale, and stop condition are distinct.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** Headline and decision use larger roles; arm labels, axis ticks, and evidence notes are secondary. Trend and distribution are grouped under Analysis, with interpretation and decision below.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** Staged-flux effect leads; standard train remains the control. Two editable series preserve input order and identity. Paired blocks remain visible rather than being collapsed into an unsupported causal claim.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Qualified. Narrow views retain both arms, the seasonal limitation, and the stop rule; the evidence table is horizontally discoverable.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** The early divergence may be read as seasonal proof; the report explicitly limits generalization.

## D — Corridor 7 Signaling Operations Review

- **Person, context, task, decision:** A rail operations controller deciding release status for Corridor 7; continue supervised East Junction release and retest relay/axle-counter evidence.
- **Current truth:** Availability recovered from 98.1% to 99.2%, yet East Junction accounts for 14 of 19 resets. A stale channel may hide an event; the overnight window is verification, not proof of durable repair.
- **Character / negative identity:** A shift handoff with an explicit uncertainty path; it avoids an all-green KPI report or an irrelevant semiconductor SPC placeholder.
- **Density and hierarchy:** Dense. Availability trend, shift interpretation, response path, six-row asset register, evidence, risk, named owner, and performance indicators support action.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** Short section headings separate analysis, flow, evidence, decision, and delivery. The narrative rectangle was expanded using Guided authoring after the first PPTX export reported text overflow; the full content then exported and survived canonical reload.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** East Junction’s reset condition is primary; aggregate availability is supporting. Blue trend and neutral evidence table preserve reading hierarchy; “Watch/Stable/Changed/Open/Uncertain” remain text as well as state cues.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Operational and conditional. The flow reads left-to-right on desktop, as an arrowed serpentine at 390px, and vertically at 320px. Table overflow is discoverable.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** Aggregate improvement could obscure a local recurrence; the report explicitly prevents release based on aggregate availability alone.

## E — Tidal Gate Pump Fleet Storm Readiness (fresh dense holdout)

- **Person, context, task, decision:** A storm-response fleet lead deciding which pumping stations to stage before a coastal weather window; stage East Sluice and Delta Yard, hold Marsh Gate for inspection and reconciliation.
- **Current truth:** Marsh Gate had the highest sustained load/vibration spread; East Sluice telemetry remained below intake threshold after a sensor swap; historian and local-counter records conflict for one interval.
- **Character / negative identity:** A dense field-readiness brief with an explicit hold; it avoids treating the highest reading as sufficient evidence for blanket fleet action.
- **Density and hierarchy:** Very high. Fleet readiness and load comparison lead into 48 observations across three stations, a station distribution, narrative, process flow, four-row evidence table, and conditional risk/next step.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** Primary load series and distribution are grouped; long provenance and sensor caveat sit in narrative/evidence surfaces; identifiers remain in the wide table.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** The three station series are distinct and ordered; box plots summarize vibration by station; status and source conflict are textual. R5 XML confirms all 48 line-series values and 16 categories per station remain cached.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Direct and safety-conscious. At 320px the diagram stacks, the full hold rationale and release action remain visible, and the table announces horizontal navigation.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** A reader could confuse a historian/local-counter conflict with a confirmed pump fault; the report labels it contested and ties action to verification.

## F — Saffron Link Cold-Chain Hub Decision (fresh sparse holdout)

- **Person, context, task, decision:** A cold-chain network operator deciding whether to trial a citrus micro-hub before the late sailing window; approve one reversible Harbor East week with a 6.0°C/20-minute stop condition.
- **Current truth:** Median dwell improved from 18 h to 14.2 h across four lanes; temperature stayed below contract limits, but humidity is 3–5 points high after calibration and four lanes are too few for network-wide savings.
- **Character / negative identity:** A sparse decision-led read with explicit reversibility; it avoids manufacturing certainty from four routes or hiding the fallback plan.
- **Density and hierarchy:** Sparse. A one-sentence headline leads, two small charts and short interpretation support it, then evidence, conditional risk, next step, and baseline comparison close the decision.
- **Typography, spacing, grouping, surfaces:** Large decision and KPI figures, standard narrative text, restrained chart labels. Support content is grouped beside the lead chart at desktop and becomes ordered blocks on narrow devices.
- **Feature/support, color, data treatment:** Harbor East activation is primary; four destination medians and temperature distribution support it. Blue plot and risk/next-step surfaces are consistent; the evidence table retains sensor-note details.
- **Tone and responsive recomposition:** Reversible and explicit. At 320/360/390px values stay atomic, rationale and fallback remain visible, and the table has keyboard/touch overflow cues.
- **Likely misunderstanding risk:** The 14.2 h summary could be generalized to every lane; four destination rows and the limited-sample caveat remain visible. PPTX low-density category thinning is separately recorded.
